# Version 2: "The week" — one cinematic scroll

Idea: the whole product in one continuous shot, scrolled like a film. One note, one week, every AI.
The camera is the storyteller: it pushes in on what's being written and pulls out to show who wrote it.
Truth: an AI reads or writes the note when you ask it; every edit is credited; you can undo.

Page: hero (words + the note, closed-up, resting) → the pinned film (~5 screens) → apps → feels like home → close.

## The film (pinned, scroll-scrubbed, each beat has a finished resting pose)
0. Rest: big note "Running plan" centred, empty except title + "Half marathon, 30 Nov". Caption top-left: "Monday".
1. MONDAY — ChatGPT. A ChatGPT window slides in from the left (min-jerk), you ask "Plan 4 runs a week for me".
   Four lines write themselves into the note one after another; a ChatGPT mark lands on the block.
   Caption: "Tell one AI." 
2. WEDNESDAY — Claude. Camera drifts right; ChatGPT window slides out left, a Claude window in from the right:
   "What should I run today?" A thin amber thread draws from the Wednesday line to Claude; Claude answers.
   Caption: "Every AI reads the same notebook."
3. FRIDAY — the check-in. Claude: "How did my week go?" Mon and Wed tick (weighted tick + small bump);
   Thursday's line lifts, slides down past Saturday and settles as "Sun · moved from Thursday", Claude mark.
   Caption: "Plans don't die here."
4. SUNDAY — you. A cursor ticks Sunday's run yourself. Week strip shows 4/4. Caption: "You stay in charge."
5. Pull out: windows leave, the note shrinks onto the paper desk (the desk art fades up behind it),
   lands like a page in a notebook. Caption: "The notebook you and your AI share."

Craft: pure function of progress p∈[0,1]; transforms only; min-jerk travel; no layout shift; type
writes at a natural pace; Reduce Motion → final pose; phones → same beats, stacked, no pin (played on view).
Day strip (Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun) along the top shows where we are, scrubbable.
