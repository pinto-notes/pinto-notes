# Version 3: "Paper" — the picture book

Idea: our signature (cut-paper art) carries the page. Each benefit is one full-width paper scene, drawn
for it, with the headline in the calm left third and one small real piece of the app laid into the scene
as proof (a credit chip, a ticked line, an imported count). Calm, warm, unlike any AI-SaaS page.

1. Hero: open notebook, three paper speech bubbles threaded to the page. "The notebook you and your AI share."
   Proof: three small credit chips (ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code) pinned near the bubbles.
2. Memory: two bubbles threading into one card. "Stop re-explaining yourself." Proof: a mini Claude reply that
   quotes the plan ChatGPT saved.
3. Week: a week strip, one tile moving to Sunday. Dark-tinted band version. "Plans don't die here."
   Proof: "Claude moved Thursday's run to Sunday" chip.
4. Pop-up: a page folding up into an app. "A note can be an app." Proof: the real habit ring chip.
5. Home: legal pad → notebook. "Feels like Apple Notes. Bring yours over." Proof: "1,284 notes brought over".
6. Close: dark band, the closed notebook with the leaf. "Start your notebook."

Motion: one quiet parallax per scene (art drifts ≤40px slower than scroll), proof chips settle once in view
(overshoot spring, 380ms), everything finished at rest. Phones: art above, words below, no parallax.
