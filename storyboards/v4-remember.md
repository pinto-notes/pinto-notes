# Version 4: "It remembers" — problem first, huge type

Idea: lead with the pain everyone with an AI has felt. Statements fill the screen, one at a time, like a
keynote; each is followed by the proof, small and real. Fewer words than any other version.

1. Hero (dark leaf brown, full screen): "Every AI you use forgets you."  … then, as you scroll a little,
   the line below it lands: "Your notebook doesn't." (cream on the marker). Visual: three chat bubbles
   (ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code) on the left fade to grey "forgotten"; one line from each is caught into
   the Pinto note on the right, which stays crisp. Download under it.
2. "Tell it once." Big type left; right: a chat in Claude quoting what you told ChatGPT last week.
3. "Ask how it's going." Big type; the Friday check-in, plan updated, Claude credited.
4. "Watch a note become an app." The fanned template cards (rest on the app).
5. "It's still just notes." Calm cream, the real import, Apple Notes comfort, the trust row.
6. Close: "Give your AIs a memory." Download.

Motion: the hero's forget/remember is scroll-scrubbed (≈1.2 screens), then the page scrolls normally.
Statements arrive once (blur-in, 450ms) and are already there at rest for fast scrollers.
