## Ideas
Anything worth a post, one line each, added the moment you think of it. Once one has a date, it moves to the calendar as Drafting.

## Voice
- Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Show the work: a screenshot or a number beats an adjective.
- End with a question, not a sales pitch.
