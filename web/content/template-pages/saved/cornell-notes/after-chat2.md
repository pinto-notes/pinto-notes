### Supply and demand, 17 November

| Cue | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Which way does the demand curve slope, and why? | Down: higher price, people buy less |
| Which way does the supply curve slope? | Up |
| Where is equilibrium? | Where the supply and demand curves cross |
| What shifts the demand curve? | Income, tastes, price of substitutes |
| Does a change in the good's own price shift demand? | No, it moves along the curve, it doesn't shift it |
| What does a price ceiling below equilibrium cause? | A shortage |

**Summary:** Demand slopes down and supply slopes up, and the market settles at equilibrium where they cross. Things like income, tastes and the price of substitutes shift the demand curve, but a change in the good's own price only moves along it. A price ceiling set below equilibrium leads to a shortage.
