**Description:**

```
Pinto Notes is a simple notes app your AI can use.

It works like the notes app you already know, with folders, checklists, tables and photos. The difference is that ChatGPT and Claude can read and update your notes when you let them.

ASK YOUR AI, IT'S IN YOUR NOTES
• "Add oat milk to my groceries." It's on your phone a second later.
• "What did we decide about the Lisbon hotel?" Your AI reads the note and answers.
• "Turn this meeting into a checklist in Work." Done, on every device.
• Every line an AI changes is highlighted, with Undo next to it.

A NOTES APP FIRST
• Checklists that move ticked items to the bottom
• Headings, bullets, numbered lists and quotes
• Tables you edit cell by cell
• Photos, PDFs and files in your notes and folders
• Sub-notes that keep details one tap away
• Keep writing offline. Changes sync when you're back.
• Lock a note with a password and Face ID

ENCRYPTED END TO END
Your notes are encrypted on your iPhone and Mac before they sync. We can't read them. Two things work differently. An AI you connect reads the notes it asks for through our server. A note you share as a web page is readable by anyone with the link.

YOU DECIDE WHO GETS IN
• You approve every AI connection on your device, read only or read and edit
• Disconnect an AI at any time
• Every change keeps the earlier version, so you can go back
• Share a note as a web page and stop sharing whenever you like
• Delete your account and everything in it from Settings

BRING YOUR NOTES
Import from Evernote, Google Keep, or Markdown and text files. On the Mac, import from Apple Notes in one step.

No ads and no tracking. Open source. 2 GB of storage.

Sign in with Apple, Google or email. Connecting ChatGPT or Claude needs your own account with that service.

Pinto Notes was called Amber Notes until October 2026.
```

## EU Digital Services Act: trader status (Emil decides)

App Store Connect asks every developer distributing in the EU whether they're a **trader**
(Business → Agreements, or the prompt on the app's page).

- **Not a trader** fits a free app made by an individual, outside a trade or profession, with no
  payments, ads or commercial purpose. Then no address or phone number is shown on the App Store.
  Apple may still ask you to confirm.
- **Trader** is required if you make money from it (now or through it, e.g. promoting a business).
  Then your address, phone number and email are shown publicly on the product page in the EU.

If you answer "not a trader", the app stays available in the EU. If Pinto Notes is ever run as part
of Norditech or Incredible, or becomes paid, switch to trader.

Clicks: App Store Connect → Business → (your account) → Digital Services Act → **Declare trader
status** → choose, then confirm.
